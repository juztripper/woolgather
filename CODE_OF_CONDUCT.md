# Code of conduct

woolgather is built around patient, inspectable thinking. Its community should work the same way.

## Our standards

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent while discussing the actual impact of a change. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience. Give credit, protect privacy, and make room for people to reconsider an idea without embarrassment.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information or project content.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive.

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@rippersgames.com](mailto:support@rippersgames.com). Reports will be handled with discretion; conflicts of interest will be avoided where practical.
